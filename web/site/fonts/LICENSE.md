# Fonts

Both typefaces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are bundled
so the kiosk renders correctly with no internet connection.

- **Young Serif** by Bastien Sozeau (display: addresses, numbers, headings)
- **Public Sans** by the U.S. Web Design System (body text and forms)

Files taken from the Fontsource distributions (`@fontsource/young-serif`, `@fontsource/public-sans`), Latin subset.
Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org
